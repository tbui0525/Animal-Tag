D-3: Flippable Squid: The Flippable Squid possesses two distinct abilities. Prior to the activation of any specific ability, a mandatory “No ability” phase must be executed. The state of the Flippable Squid determines which ability the player has when in possession of the Flippable Squid.

D3-1. Blue: The blue side of the squid cancels all aquatic/semiaquatic creature abilities.Should the player holding the white unicorn come into contact with a sabotage effect, said sabotage shall not be applied to the current player; instead, the original individual subject to the sabotage shall remain sabotaged.
D3-2. Dark: The dark side of the squid temporarily removes vision of the player designated as "It" for a duration of five (5) seconds. To mitigate the possibility of excessive or disruptive use, the activation of this ability is subject to a mandatory cooldown period of one minute (60 seconds). This one-minute cooldown shall be uniformly applied to all participants, preventing any player from initiating a subsequent "Blind" immediately following the termination of a preceding "Blind" effect.
