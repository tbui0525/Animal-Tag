The Wolf possesses a proximal ability and is exempt from the requirement of verbal announcement. The regional ability of the wolf dictates that a player in possession of the wolf may not be tagged or subjected to sabotage if the player(s) designated as "It" attempts to attack from within 3 feet of the player in possession of the Wolf. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if the player possessing the Wolf intentionally situates himself/herself within that range, the Wolf ability will be deemed null and void as agreed upon by whoever possesses "It." Likewise, under cases such that the exact distance between targeter and targetted could not be deduced with sufficient accuracy, the leeway favors the thrower.
